The control-plane service is {{ include "scip.fullname" . }}.

The chart does not create credentials. Before installation, create
Secret/{{ .Values.runtimeSecretName }} with these keys:

  SCIP_DATABASE_URL
  SCIP_JWT_SIGNING_KEY

Ingress TLS is terminated with the Secret referenced by ingress.tls. Configure
encrypted PostgreSQL transport in SCIP_DATABASE_URL and restrict Secret access
through your cluster's external secret delivery system.
