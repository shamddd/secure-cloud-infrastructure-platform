# Code of conduct

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free project environment. Be
respectful, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims with evidence, give actionable
feedback, and protect private or sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained personal attacks are
not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed
to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub
profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Maintainers will handle reports as
confidentially and promptly as practical, while avoiding conflicts of interest.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
